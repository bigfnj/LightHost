Light Host — level metering, three options

Drawn at the real window width of 520 px, in the application's own palette and accent colour (#4a9eff, kAccent). Every figure shown is a real measurement from today, so each panel depicts a state the app was actually in and could not show you.

What happened next. All of this shipped in 5.1.0. A and B became the two device meters and the latching CLIP badge; C became SignalViewPanel -- the side column, the Δ column, and probes that exist only while the panel is open. D was declined for the reason in its own caption. The four signal colours in the stylesheet are pinned to the source and all four still match; the chrome colours are not, and never were.
